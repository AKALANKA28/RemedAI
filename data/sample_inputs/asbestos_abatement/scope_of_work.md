## Scope of Work

- Establish containment zones and negative pressure enclosures.
- Remove and package asbestos-containing material per regulatory guidelines.
- Provide on-site decontamination units and worker PPE management.
- Perform air monitoring and clearance testing via certified laboratory.
- Transport hazardous waste to a licensed disposal facility.
- Maintain incident response readiness during removal activities.
- Provide weekly progress updates and final clearance certificates.
