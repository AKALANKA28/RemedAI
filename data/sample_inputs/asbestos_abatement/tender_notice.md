# Tender Notice

**Issuer:** National Industrial Estates Board
**Project title:** Asbestos abatement and hazardous waste clearance for Rivergate Industrial Park
**Submission deadline:** 2026-06-20
**Estimated budget:** LKR 140,000,000
**Contract duration:** 6 months

## Background

The Board seeks a licensed asbestos removal contractor to execute containment, abatement, and hazardous waste disposal across three industrial warehouses, including air monitoring and clearance certification.

## Mandatory Requirements

- REQ-01: Bidder must hold an active asbestos removal contractor license.
- REQ-02: Bidder shall demonstrate at least 3 asbestos abatement projects completed in the last 5 years.
- REQ-03: Bidder must provide a certified asbestos supervisor and safety officer on site.
- REQ-04: Bidder must provide HEPA-filtered negative pressure units and an air monitoring plan.
- REQ-05: Bidder must maintain licensed hazardous waste transport and disposal arrangements.
- REQ-06: Bidder must maintain a worker medical surveillance program.
- REQ-07: Bidder shall mobilize within 21 calendar days from Notice to Proceed.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Technical methodology and containment controls.
- Relevant asbestos abatement experience.
- Safety management and compliance readiness.
- Schedule feasibility and price reasonableness.

## Clarifications

- Air monitoring may be delivered through an accredited subcontracted laboratory.
- Disposal site letters of acceptance must be included in the submission.
