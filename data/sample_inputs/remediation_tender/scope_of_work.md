# Scope of Work

1. Excavate and isolate contaminated soil hotspots.
2. Coordinate approved transport and disposal of hazardous material.
3. Install monitoring wells and integrate telemetry into the Authority SCADA environment.
4. Collect groundwater and soil samples under documented chain-of-custody controls.
5. Maintain operational readiness for incident containment during excavation shifts.
6. Produce monthly progress maps and a final remediation close-out pack.
