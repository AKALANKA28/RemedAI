# Tender Notice

**Issuer:** Central Environmental Restoration Authority
**Project title:** Emergency remediation and groundwater monitoring for the Kurana Chemical Storage Site
**Submission deadline:** 2026-05-14
**Estimated budget:** LKR 185,000,000
**Contract duration:** 10 months

## Background
The Authority seeks a qualified contractor to execute hazardous hotspot remediation, install and operate telemetry-enabled groundwater monitoring wells, and maintain environmental incident response readiness during active remediation.

## Mandatory Requirements
- REQ-01: Bidder must hold a valid ISO 14001 certification.
- REQ-02: Bidder shall demonstrate at least 3 similar projects completed within the last 5 years involving remediation and/or groundwater monitoring.
- REQ-03: Bidder must demonstrate 24x7 environmental incident response coverage during active excavation windows.
- REQ-04: Bidder shall mobilize a field team within 14 calendar days from Notice to Proceed.
- REQ-05: Bidder must maintain laboratory sample chain-of-custody procedures.
- REQ-06: Bidder must support SCADA-compatible telemetry integration for groundwater instrumentation.
- REQ-07: Bidder must provide updated drone-based site mapping for progress verification.

## Evaluation Criteria
- Technical methodology and environmental controls.
- Relevant project experience.
- Team readiness and mobilization feasibility.
- Price reasonableness.

## Clarifications
- Emergency response coverage may be delivered through an approved specialist partner, subject to written commitment.
- Drone mapping evidence may come from a named subcontractor if credentials are attached.
