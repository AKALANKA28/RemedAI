# Tender Notice

**Issuer:** Northern River Basin Authority
**Project title:** Riverbank diesel spill remediation and monitoring at the Galena Reach
**Submission deadline:** 2026-06-03
**Estimated budget:** LKR 92,000,000
**Contract duration:** 8 months

## Background

The Authority seeks a qualified contractor to remediate riverbank sediment impacted by a diesel spill, install telemetry-enabled monitoring wells, and maintain spill response readiness during active excavation.

## Mandatory Requirements

- REQ-01: Bidder must hold a valid ISO 14001 certification.
- REQ-02: Bidder shall demonstrate at least 2 similar riverbank remediation projects completed in the last 5 years.
- REQ-03: Bidder must provide 24x7 spill response coverage during active excavation.
- REQ-04: Bidder shall mobilize a field team within 10 calendar days from Notice to Proceed.
- REQ-05: Bidder must maintain chain-of-custody procedures for all sampling.
- REQ-06: Bidder must integrate turbidity and groundwater telemetry into the Authority SCADA system.
- REQ-07: Bidder must submit a water safety plan for boat-based operations.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Technical methodology for sediment control and dewatering.
- Relevant riverbank remediation experience.
- Response readiness and mobilization feasibility.
- Price reasonableness.

## Clarifications

- Spill response coverage may be delivered through an approved specialist partner with a commitment letter.
- Telemetry integration may use a nominated instrumentation vendor.
