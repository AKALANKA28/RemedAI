## Scope of Work

- Isolate and remove diesel-contaminated riverbank soil and sediment.
- Install silt curtains and temporary flow controls to prevent downstream turbidity.
- Dewater excavations and manage treatment of recovered water.
- Install groundwater monitoring wells with telemetry for level and turbidity.
- Collect soil and water samples under chain-of-custody controls.
- Maintain 24x7 spill response readiness during active works.
- Provide weekly progress maps and a final remediation close-out report.
