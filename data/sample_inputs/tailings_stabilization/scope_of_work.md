## Scope of Work

- Regrade tailings dam slopes and install erosion controls.
- Install piezometers and monitoring wells with telemetry reporting.
- Construct diversion channels and stormwater controls.
- Collect soil and water samples under chain-of-custody controls.
- Update and exercise an emergency action plan with site staff.
- Establish vegetation cover and long-term maintenance plan.
- Deliver monthly geotechnical monitoring reports and a close-out dossier.
