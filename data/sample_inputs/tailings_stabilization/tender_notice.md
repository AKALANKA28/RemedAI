# Tender Notice

**Issuer:** State Mining Rehabilitation Unit
**Project title:** Tailings dam stabilization and groundwater protection program at the Laksiri Mine
**Submission deadline:** 2026-07-05
**Estimated budget:** LKR 310,000,000
**Contract duration:** 14 months

## Background

The Unit seeks a contractor to stabilize the Laksiri Mine tailings dam, install geotechnical monitoring instrumentation, and execute groundwater protection controls with an updated emergency action plan.

## Mandatory Requirements

- REQ-01: Bidder must provide a lead geotechnical engineer with at least 10 years of experience.
- REQ-02: Bidder shall demonstrate at least 2 tailings dam stabilization projects completed in the last 7 years.
- REQ-03: Bidder must submit an emergency action plan aligned to national dam safety guidelines.
- REQ-04: Bidder must provide instrumentation telemetry compatible with the agency monitoring portal.
- REQ-05: Bidder must maintain chain-of-custody sampling and a QA/QC plan.
- REQ-06: Bidder shall mobilize within 30 calendar days from Notice to Proceed.
- REQ-07: Bidder must provide an environmental management plan addressing dust and runoff.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Geotechnical methodology and stabilization design.
- Risk controls and safety planning.
- Relevant tailings dam experience.
- Schedule feasibility and price reasonableness.

## Clarifications

- Instrumentation may be provided through a nominated vendor with integration documentation.
- The emergency action plan may be updated from the site draft provided at kickoff.
